SHOR MEANS NOISE.

In Hindi, "Shor" translates directly to raw, unapologetic sound. The hum of metro trains, honking auto-rickshaws, underground basement gigs, and street markets that pulse past midnight. We build footwear tuned to that frequency.

THE INDIAN STREETWEAR DIALECT

For decades, sneaker culture in India has been shaped by silhouettes designed elsewhere, manufactured across global supply chains, and sold at prices that often leave little room for individuality.

SHORS was founded to challenge that narrative. We bring a distinctly Indian perspective to streetwear footwear, combining raw architectural design, functional details, and a visual language inspired by the streets we call home.

From tropical downpours and dusty concrete sidewalks to the everyday rhythm of urban life, every design begins with a simple question: what should a sneaker built around our streets actually look and feel like?

We focus on considered silhouettes, durable materials, and distinctive finishes. Rather than operating our own manufacturing facilities, we collaborate with external footwear manufacturers and specialist production partners to bring our designs to life, while keeping our attention on the creative direction, product development, and identity of every release.

DESIGN-LED PRODUCTION MODEL

We believe great footwear starts with a strong idea, not a factory. At SHORS, our focus is on developing distinctive silhouettes, refining design details, and building a consistent identity across every release.

Our designs are developed in-house and brought to production through carefully selected third-party footwear manufacturers. We work with external partners for manufacturing, material sourcing, and production execution, allowing us to focus on what defines SHORS: design, functionality, and streetwear culture.

We aim to build through considered releases rather than chasing volume for its own sake. Each collection is guided by our design direction, with production specifications and quality expectations communicated to our manufacturing partners.

Designed by SHORS. Produced with specialist partners. Built for the streets.

MEASURE THE NOISE

SHORS footwear follows standard Indian (IND) and British (UK) sizing. Follow our 3-step at-home guide to calibrate your foot length in centimeters.

01

PLACE HEEL AGAINST WALL

Tape a blank sheet of white paper to a flat, hard floor. Stand upright with your heel firmly pressed against a vertical wall, wearing the socks you plan to use.

02

MARK LONGEST TOE

Hold a pencil vertically (at a 90-degree angle) and draw a precise line at the furthest tip of your longest toe (either the big toe or second toe).

03

COMPARE CENTIMETERS

Use a standard ruler to measure the distance in centimeters (cm) from the edge of the paper to your pencil mark. Compare against our sizing matrix below.

SNEAKERS ARE MORE THAN JUST FOOTWEAR

Sneakers have become a big part of everyday fashion. For many people, especially younger shoppers, a pair of sneakers is not simply something worn for comfort. It is one of the easiest ways to show personal style. The right pair can change the feel of an outfit without requiring a complete change of wardrobe.

This is one reason streetwear sneakers for men have become so popular. Streetwear is generally relaxed, expressive and easy to adapt to everyday life. It can include oversized clothing, graphic T-shirts, hoodies, cargo trousers, denim, jackets and, of course, sneakers. At the same time, there are so many designs available that choosing a pair can become confusing.

A good sneaker should look good, but it should also make sense for the way you actually live. Before buying a new pair, it is worth thinking about style, comfort, versatility and how often you are likely to wear them. Here are some simple things to consider when choosing your next pair.

01

1. Start With Your Own Style

The first thing to think about is your personal style. There is no single definition of streetwear. Some people like simple outfits and neutral colours, while others prefer bold graphics, unusual shapes and statement pieces.

Your sneakers should work with the clothes you already enjoy wearing. If you normally wear oversized T-shirts, relaxed trousers and hoodies, a chunkier sneaker can fit naturally into that wardrobe. If your outfits are more minimal, a cleaner sneaker may be easier to style.

This is especially useful when looking for stylish sneakers for men. A sneaker does not have to follow every trend to look good. Sometimes the best choice is a design that feels like you and can be worn in several different ways.

The main idea is simple: choose sneakers because you genuinely like them, not only because they are popular at the moment.

02

2. Think About Where You Will Wear Them

Your daily routine should also influence your choice. A student who walks around campus every day may need something different from someone who mainly wears sneakers when going out with friends.

Think about how you will actually use the pair. Will you wear them to college, while travelling, while meeting friends, while shopping, or during normal days around the city? If you expect to wear them regularly, comfort and versatility become more important.

Casual sneakers for men are useful because they can fit into many parts of everyday life. A versatile pair can work with jeans, cargos, relaxed trousers, shorts and other casual clothing. Instead of buying a sneaker that only works with one outfit, look for something that gives you several options.

The more situations a pair can handle, the more likely it is to become a regular part of your wardrobe.

03

3. Do Not Ignore Comfort

Streetwear is strongly connected with appearance, but comfort still matters. A sneaker can look great in a picture and still be uncomfortable after wearing it for several hours.

When looking at comfortable sneakers for men, pay attention to the fit. Your toes should have enough room to move, while the heel should feel secure. The shoe should not feel excessively tight or loose.

This matters even more if the sneaker is going to be used every day. Walking between classes, travelling across a city, shopping or spending an afternoon with friends can involve a lot more movement than expected.

A good everyday sneaker should allow you to focus on what you are doing instead of thinking about your feet. Style is important, but there is no point in owning a great-looking pair that you avoid wearing because it is uncomfortable.

"A good everyday sneaker should allow you to focus on what you are doing instead of thinking about your feet. Style is important, but there is no point in owning a great-looking pair that you avoid wearing because it is uncomfortable."

04

4. Look at the Whole Design, Not Just the Colour

Colour is usually the first thing people notice, but the overall shape of a sneaker can change an outfit just as much. Two pairs can have similar colours but create completely different looks because their silhouettes are different.

Slimmer sneakers can create a cleaner and simpler appearance. Chunkier silhouettes often work well with relaxed streetwear because they match the proportions of oversized clothing and wider trousers.

When choosing men's streetwear sneakers, consider the shape, sole, materials and overall proportions. Think about whether the design fits the clothes you normally wear.

If your wardrobe is already colourful, a neutral sneaker may give the outfit some balance. If your clothing is mostly simple, a more distinctive sneaker can become the main focus.

There is no universal right answer here. The best choice depends on your wardrobe and the kind of look you want to create.

05

5. Make Sure the Sneakers Work With Your Wardrobe

A sneaker becomes much more useful when it works with several outfits. Before buying a pair, think about the clothes you already own.

Can you wear the sneakers with your favourite jeans? Do they work with cargo trousers? Would they look good with shorts? Can you pair them with an oversized T-shirt, hoodie or jacket?

These questions help you judge whether a sneaker is versatile rather than simply attractive on its own.

For people looking for sneakers for men, versatility can also make the purchase more practical. A pair that works with five or six outfits is likely to be worn much more often than a pair that only matches one specific look.

This does not mean that every sneaker needs to be plain. A bold design can still be versatile if its colours and overall shape work with the rest of your wardrobe.

06

6. Do Not Follow Every Trend

Sneaker trends can change quickly. A style that is everywhere this month may become less common later. There is nothing wrong with enjoying trends, but buying something only because everyone else has it is not always the best decision.

Instead, ask yourself whether you would still wear the sneaker if the trend disappeared. If the answer is yes, it is probably a better addition to your wardrobe.

Streetwear has always had a strong connection with individuality. The idea is not simply to copy another person's outfit. It is to take inspiration and make it your own.

This is where a brand like SHORS can fit naturally. A sneaker should give people room to express their own style instead of forcing everyone into exactly the same look.

"Streetwear has always had a strong connection with individuality. The idea is not simply to copy another person's outfit. It is to take inspiration and make it your own."

07

7. Think About Everyday Use and Maintenance

If you want everyday sneakers for men, think about how much use the pair will receive. Daily footwear can experience a lot of wear, especially if you walk frequently or live in a busy city.

The colour and material can also affect how easy the sneakers are to maintain. A very light pair may show dirt more easily, while darker colours can be more forgiving for everyday use.

This does not mean you should always choose a dark sneaker. It simply means you should think realistically about your routine.

Taking care of your sneakers also helps them last longer. Basic cleaning, storing them properly and avoiding unnecessary exposure to conditions they are not designed for can make a difference.

A sneaker that fits your lifestyle is usually more satisfying than one that looks good but is difficult to use in your normal routine.

08

8. Use Sneakers to Build the Outfit

Sneaker styling for men does not have to be complicated. Start with the overall balance of the outfit.

If the sneakers are bold, keeping the rest of the outfit relatively simple can make them stand out. If the sneakers are minimal, you can use other parts of the outfit, such as a jacket, graphic T-shirt or accessories, to add more personality.

Proportions are also important. Wider trousers and oversized tops can work well with chunkier sneakers, while slimmer trousers can pair naturally with more streamlined footwear.

The best part about streetwear is that there is room to experiment. You can try different combinations and see what feels natural. There is no need to follow a strict formula.

09

9. Choose a Pair That Feels Like You

At the end of the day, the best streetwear sneakers for men are not necessarily the pair that is most expensive, most popular or most heavily promoted. They are the pair that fits your lifestyle and feels right when you wear them.

Style, comfort and versatility should work together. You should be able to look at the sneaker and imagine it with the clothes you already own. You should also be comfortable enough to wear it for the amount of time your routine requires.

SHORS is built around this idea of making sneakers part of everyday expression. A sneaker can be functional while still having personality. It can be comfortable without looking boring and stylish without being difficult to wear.

The goal is not to find a sneaker that everyone else likes. The goal is to find a sneaker that makes sense for you.

★

Final Thoughts

Choosing the right sneakers does not have to be complicated. Start by understanding your own style, then think about where you will wear the sneakers, how comfortable they are, how well they fit your wardrobe and whether the design feels like something you will continue to enjoy.

Streetwear gives people the freedom to experiment. You can keep things minimal, go for a bold look or find something somewhere in between. What matters most is that the final outfit feels natural.

The next time you look for a new pair of sneakers, do not ask only, "Does this look good?"

Ask whether it works with your lifestyle, your wardrobe and your personality.

The right pair of sneakers is not simply another item in your wardrobe. It can become part of your everyday style and one of the easiest ways to show a little bit of who you are. That is the idea behind SHORS: sneakers made to fit everyday life while still giving people the freedom to express themselves.
